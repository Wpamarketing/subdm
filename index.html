FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need hosting? +
No, we handle everything for you. We connect your website to third-party hosting platforms and manage all the technical setup. You just provide your details.
Can I use my own domain? +
Yes! You can connect your own custom domain to your subdm.site subdomain. We handle the DNS setup for you.
Is SSL certificate included? +
100% included. All SubDM websites come with a free SSL certificate for secure HTTPS connections. Your visitors' data is always protected.
How long does setup take? +
24–48 hours from the time you place your order. We'll set up your website, configure your subdomain, and get everything live so you can start using it immediately.
Can I upgrade or modify my plan later? +
Absolutely! You can upgrade to a higher plan, customize your design, or add new features anytime. We're here to grow with you.
What if I'm not satisfied? +
We stand behind our work. Contact our support team within 7 days if you have concerns, and we'll fix it for free or offer a refund.
